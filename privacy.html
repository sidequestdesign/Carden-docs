Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

Last Updated: September 2026 / 最近更新：2026年9月

1. Overview / 概述

MarCoo is designed as a local-first application. We believe your personal bookmarks, design inspiration, notes, and browsing data belong solely to you. MarCoo does NOT collect, track, or share any personal identifiable information.

MarCoo 是一款本地优先（Local-First）的应用。我们坚信您的书签、设计灵感、备忘笔记与网页浏览数据完全属于您自己。MarCoo 不会收集、追踪或共享任何用户的个人身份信息。

2. Data Storage / 数据存储

All your links, images, tags, folders, and notes are stored locally on your device within your designated .readinglibrary packages and local SQLite databases. We do not operate proprietary cloud servers to host or sync your personal content without your direct configuration.

您的所有链接、图片素材、标签、文件夹及微笔记均存储在您设备本地的 .readinglibrary 资源库及 SQLite 数据库中。我们不运营任何专属云端服务器来存储或同步您的个人数据。

3. Network Requests & Web Parsing / 网络请求与网页解析

MarCoo only initiates network requests when you explicitly request it to parse a webpage's title, OpenGraph preview metadata, or download images you choose to save. These requests are made directly from your Mac to the respective third-party website, with no intermediary proxy operated by us logging your traffic.

仅在您主动收藏或解析某个网页时，MarCoo 才会向目标网站发送网络请求以获取标题、预览图及元数据。这些网络请求由您的 Mac 直接与目标网站通信，我们不会通过任何中转代理服务器记录您的流量或访问历史。

4. Safari Extension / Safari 扩展

The MarCoo Safari Extension only accesses the active browser tab when you click or trigger an action (such as clipping a link or opening the image picker). It does NOT continuously track your browsing activity across the web.

MarCoo 的 Safari 扩展仅在您主动点击或触发操作（如保存当前页面、打开网页图片提取器）时才访问当前标签页。它绝不会在后台持续监控或追踪您的全网浏览行为。

5. In-App Purchases / 应用内购买

All subscriptions and in-app purchases are processed directly by Apple via the App Store (StoreKit). We do not collect, view, or store your credit card details or financial information.

所有订阅与应用内购买均由 Apple 官方通过 App Store (StoreKit) 进行安全处理。我们不会收集、接触或存储您的任何银行卡或财务结算信息。
